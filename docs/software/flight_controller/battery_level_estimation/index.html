Battery Level Estimation

The battery level is estimated by feeding the voltage and current through a Kalman Filter based on the Thevenin 1RC Model.

Battery Model

The battery model is the Thevenin 1RC Model :

\[V(t) = V_{oc}(t) - I(t)R_{int} - V_{pol}(t)\]
\[\frac{dV_{pol}}{dt} = \frac{I(t)}{C_{pol}} - \frac{V_{pol}}{R_{pol}C_{pol}}\]

  • \(V(t)\) : The measured voltage of the battery

  • \(I(t)\) : The measured current out of the battery

  • \(V_{oc}(t)\) : Open Circuit Voltage, the voltage that the battery would have if disconnected, this is what we are trying to estimate

  • \(R_{int}\) : The internal resistance of the battery

  • \(V_{pol}(t)\) : The polarisation voltage

  • \(R_{pol}\) : The polarisation resistance

  • \(C_{pol}\) : The polarisation capacitance

Kalman Filter

The kalman filter is based on the wikipedia description and is configured as follows :

State (x) : \(\begin{bmatrix} V_{oc}(t) \\ V_{pol}(t) \end{bmatrix}\)

Command (u) : \(\begin{bmatrix} I(t) \end{bmatrix}\)

Measure (z) : \(\begin{bmatrix} V(t) + I(t)R_{int} \end{bmatrix}\)

Kalman Matrices

\[\begin{split}F = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 - \frac{dt}{R_{pol}C_{pol}} \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
\[\begin{split}B = \begin{bmatrix} -dt*\frac{dV_{OC}}{dQ} \\ \frac{dt}{C_{pol}} \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
\[\begin{split}Q = \begin{bmatrix} dt^2 \sigma_{I}^2 \frac{dV_{OC}}{dQ}^2 + \sigma_{V_{oc}}^2 & - \frac{dt^2}{C_{pol}} \sigma_{I}^2 \frac{dV_{OC}}{dQ} \\ - \frac{dt^2}{C_{pol}} \sigma_{I}^2 \frac{dV_{OC}}{dQ} & \frac{dt^2\sigma_{I}^2}{C_{pol}^2} + \sigma_{V_{pol}}^2 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
\[H = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1\end{bmatrix}\]
\[R = \begin{bmatrix} \sigma_{V}^2 + R_{int}^2 \sigma_{I}^2 \end{bmatrix}\]

Note

\(\frac{dV_{OC}}{dQ}\) corresponds to the small voltage drop caused by a small capacity usage of the battery when the open circuit voltage is \(V_{OC}\), this is calculated using Voltage-Charge lookup tables

Kalman Settings

  • \(R_{int}\) : The internal resistance of the battery

  • \(R_{pol}\) : The polarisation resistance

  • \(C_{pol}\) : The polarisation capacitance

  • \(\sigma_{V}\) : The voltage measure standard deviation

  • \(\sigma_{I}\) : The current measure standard deviation

  • \(\sigma_{V_{oc}}\) : The standard deviation of the \(V_{oc}\) process noise

  • \(\sigma_{V_{pol}}\) : The standard deviation of the \(V_{pol}\) process noise

  • \(\sigma_{0V_{oc}}\) : The initial standard deviation of the \(V_{oc}\) estimation

  • \(\sigma_{0V_{pol}}\) : The initial standard deviation of the \(V_{pol}\) estimation

  • \(CV_{LUT}\) : The charge / voltage lookup table of the battery (used to compute \(\frac{dV_{OC}}{dQ}\))

  • \(C_{max}\) : The capacity of the battery (used to compute \(\frac{dV_{OC}}{dQ}\))
